# Requirements

## FR-001 - Record Solar Energy

**Type:** Functional Requirement  
**Priority:** High  

**Description:**  
The system should record the solar energy generated by each resident from their smart meter.

**Acceptance Criteria:**  
The solar energy generated by a resident should be recorded correctly in kWh.

**Rationale:**  
This is needed to know how much solar energy and credits a resident has.


## FR-002 - View Solar Credit Balance

**Type:** Functional Requirement  
**Priority:** High  

**Description:**  
The system should allow residents to check their available solar credit balance.

**Acceptance Criteria:**  
The resident should be able to see their current solar credit balance.

**Rationale:**  
Residents need to know how many credits they have before transferring them.


## FR-003 - Transfer Solar Credits

**Type:** Functional Requirement  
**Priority:** High  

**Description:**  
The system should allow a resident to transfer extra solar credits to another resident in the same neighborhood.

**Acceptance Criteria:**  
The transferred credits should be removed from the sender's balance and added to the receiver's balance. The transfer should not happen if the sender does not have enough credits.

**Rationale:**  
This allows residents with extra solar credits to share them with other residents.


## FR-004 - Apply Credits to Electricity Bill

**Type:** Functional Requirement  
**Priority:** High  

**Description:**  
The system should apply the available solar credits to reduce the monthly electricity bill of the resident.

**Acceptance Criteria:**  
The billing amount should be reduced based on the solar credits used.

**Rationale:**  
The main benefit of solar credits is to help reduce electricity costs.


## FR-005 - Monitor Solar Activities

**Type:** Functional Requirement  
**Priority:** Medium  

**Description:**  
The Co-op Manager should be able to view the solar energy generation and credit transfers happening in the community.

**Acceptance Criteria:**  
The manager should be able to see the generation details and credit transfer details.

**Rationale:**  
This helps the manager keep track of the activities happening in the community.
